# Magic Hinge 0.9 Beta: launch ideas and draft copy

Start with a small beta launch and invite feedback before a wider launch. Every
post should explicitly say **0.9 Beta**, free, Apple silicon, and link to the
website. Avoid calling it a stable release or claiming all hardware was tested.

## 1. An eight-second loop

Show a real MacBook opening, the desktop turning to glass, then closing. Use the
approved example desktop. Overlay: “A completely unnecessary upgrade to opening
your laptop.” End card: “Magic Hinge 0.9 Beta · Free for macOS”. Make portrait and
landscape versions; include captions so it works muted. A real-device clip makes
it clear that the website animation represents a working utility.

Draft post:

> I made a completely unnecessary upgrade to opening your laptop.
>
> Magic Hinge brings the iPhone Duo screen animation to your MacBook.
> Free, open source, and now available as **0.9 Beta** for Apple silicon Macs.
>
> Warning: you may find yourself opening and closing your MacBook repeatedly.
> Try it: https://roelvangils.github.io/magic-hinge/

## 2. Ask for a specific beta test

Share with your existing Mac/developer audience. Ask for the Mac model and macOS
version, whether the sensor works, and whether sleep/wake behaves correctly.
Direct reproducible problems to GitHub Issues. This is more useful than asking
for generic likes, stars or “thoughts”.

## 3. A short behind-the-scenes post

Show the hinge sketch, an early render, and the final effect. Explain three
concrete details: reading the sensor, rendering glass, and keeping desktop images
in memory. Finish with the free beta link. This gives developers a reason to
share beyond the visual joke.

## 4. Show HN, once the public install is verified

Suggested title: “Show HN: Magic Hinge – a glass animation controlled by your MacBook lid”.
Opening comment: explain that it is a small native Swift/Metal utility, free and
open source, beta limitations, and why the simulator exists. Be available to
answer implementation questions. Show HN requires something people can try:
https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html

## 5. A small press kit for Mac writers and creators

Prepare a clean eight-second clip, three screenshots, the original app icon,
a 60-word description, requirements, and the beta limitations. Pitch the visual
story: “Makes your MacBook feel slightly more expensive.” Keep any outreach
personal and short; send only after the owner chooses recipients and authorizes it.
