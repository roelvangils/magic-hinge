# Magic Hinge 2.3.0

Magic Hinge adds a little delight to your MacBook: tilt the lid to bend, soften
and fade your desktop like frosted glass. Hold still and the effect settles back.

- A three-step welcome guide in English and Dutch, with an interactive example
  and optional Screen Recording permission.
- An interactive MacBook simulator with Air, Pro and Neo configurations, original
  Apple models downloaded and checksum-verified on demand, and an offline fallback.
- Corrected closed-lid positioning for Air and contact bounds for AR-based assets.
- Mouse, trackpad and keyboard controls, appearance options, and Reduce Motion
  support for decorative animation.
- An original app icon, optional Sparkle update checks, and local-only image
  processing. Automatic update downloads/installations remain off.

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 14.2 or later. Automatic hinge control depends
on the sensor detected in your Mac; the simulator works without one. Existing
preferences and model cache retain the original bundle identifier. Moving from
a locally signed version may require reconfirming Screen Recording access.

Release publication is held until the verification gates in release-review.md
have passed. Remove this preparation note only when publishing the final artifact.
